# REFUND POLICY

**Last Updated: Nov 27, 2025**

At Costrym, we strive to provide exceptional software and services that meet your needs. This Refund Policy outlines the terms and conditions under which you may request and receive a refund for our product. We believe in transparency and want to ensure you understand our commitment to customer satisfaction.

## 1. Refund Eligibility

### 1.1 Refund Window
- **Startup Plan Subscribers:** You may request a refund after 10 days and within 30 days of your initial purchase date provided Costrym has failed to generate actionable savings.
- **Enterprise Plan Subscribers:** You may request a refund within 14 days of the initial subscription date.

### 1.2 Valid Reasons for a Refund
Refunds will be considered for the following reasons:

- **Failure to Find Meaningful Savings:** Full refund if Costrym is unable to find savings in your company's spending (active from 10th to 30th day of initial subscription).
  - *Note:* Meaningful saving is defined as:
    - Startup Plan: No less than 100 USD per month
    - Enterprise Plan: No less than 200 USD per month

- **Technical Issues:** Service consistently fails to function as advertised despite reasonable attempts to resolve through support channels.

- **Billing Errors:** Incorrect charges, duplicate charges, or charges higher than agreed upon.

### 1.3 Reasons for Ineligibility
Refunds will not be granted for the following reasons:

- **Minimum Expense Threshold Not Met:** Users must have average business spending of 1,000 USD per month or more.

- **Incomplete Onboarding:** Refunds only valid if user completes:
  - Connecting required data sources
  - Completing spending categories
  - Enabling monitoring
  - Completing the 7-day optimization setup

- **Previous Refund Issued:** Refund guarantee is once per subscriber.

- **Fraud or Misuse:** Including false financial statements, artificial expenses, or repeated refund attempts.

## 2. Refund Review Process

All refund requests will be thoroughly reviewed within 7 days of receipt. Approved refunds will be issued within 7 days of approval. We reserve the right to deny requests that do not comply with this policy.

## 3. How to Request a Refund

1. Contact our customer support team at **accounts@budera.ai** within the refund window
2. Provide your name, email address, order/invoice number, and detailed explanation
3. Our team will review your request and respond within 7 business days

## 4. Refund Processing Time

- Approved refunds will be processed within 5-7 business days
- Refunds will be credited to your original payment method

- This Refund Policy may be updated periodically. Please review it regularly.
- This policy does not limit any consumer rights you may have under applicable law.
