# Razory - Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: August 24, 2025**

Razory ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our Chrome Extension and website. If you do not agree with the terms, please do not use Razory.

## 1. Information We Collect

* **Personal Data:** When you create an account, we may collect your name, email address, and login details.
* **Usage Data:** We collect information about your tab usage (e.g., active tabs, time spent on sites) to provide focus tracking and analytics.
* **Technical Data:** Basic device and browser information (IP address, operating system, version) for performance and security purposes.

We do **not** collect or store sensitive information beyond what is necessary for the Service.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use collected data to:

* Provide focus tracking and reports.
* Improve and personalize your experience.
* Maintain security and prevent misuse.
* Communicate important updates and support messages.

## 3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or rent your data. We may share limited information with trusted third-party providers (e.g., hosting, analytics) solely to operate the Service. Information may also be disclosed if required by law.

## 4. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely. While we use reasonable measures to protect your information, no method of storage or transmission is 100% secure.

## 5. Children’s Privacy

Razory is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## 6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights such as:

* Access and request a copy of your data.
* Request correction or deletion of your data.
* Object to or restrict processing in certain cases.

You can exercise these rights by contacting us.

## 7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. Updates will be posted with a new “Last Updated” date.
